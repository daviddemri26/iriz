Get Iriz working
the way you want.

Requirements, permissions and answers to the questions most likely to come up.

01

Requirements

Iriz requires macOS 15 or later. AI interpretation and transcription require your own paid OpenAI API key and an internet connection.

02

Permissions

Screen Recording enables visual context. Accessibility provides the active application, window title and browser URL. Microphone and Notifications are optional.

03

Privacy controls

Pause from the app or with ⇧⌘P. Add exclusions in Settings and revoke any permission in macOS System Settings.

Common questions

Quick answers.

Why is my Journal empty?+

Confirm onboarding is complete, Iriz is not paused, your OpenAI API key is valid and Screen Recording permission is granted. Iriz intentionally suppresses routine or low-value activity, so not every window change creates an event.

Where do I add my OpenAI API key?+

Open Iriz Settings, choose the OpenAI section and use Test & Save. The key is stored in macOS Keychain and requests go directly from your Mac to OpenAI.

Can I use Iriz without microphone access?+

Yes. Microphone capture is optional and can remain disabled. Screen-based journal features can still work when their required permissions and API access are enabled.

How do I stop Iriz from seeing a specific app or website?+

Open Settings and add the application bundle identifier, domain or a window-title keyword to the exclusion lists. Password managers and authentication windows are excluded automatically.

How long are screenshots and audio kept?+

Raw screenshots and audio are encrypted locally and expire after 24 hours. Structured journal retention is controlled separately in Settings.

How do I export my journal?+

Use Export in Settings and choose JSON or Markdown. Exports are not encrypted, so store them carefully. Raw screenshots, raw audio and API keys are never included.
