Descriptions.

One line

A private, searchable memory for the work that matters.

Short description

Iriz is a local-first memory for macOS that turns meaningful actions, decisions and commitments into a private, searchable journal.

Boilerplate

Iriz is a native macOS contextual memory designed to preserve the useful moments that disappear between apps, tabs and conversations. It notices meaningful changes, filters routine activity and organizes decisions, purchases, appointments and commitments into an encrypted local journal. Users can search their history, ask questions with cited sources and manage loose ends in Follow Up. Iriz never captures keystrokes, clipboard contents or camera video. Raw media is encrypted locally and expires after 24 hours.

Core messages

What makes Iriz different.

01

It remembers outcomes, not activity.

Iriz is designed to surface useful moments rather than produce surveillance-style timelines or noisy productivity scores.

02

Privacy is architectural.

The journal is encrypted locally, raw evidence expires, sensitive sources are excluded and observation can be paused instantly.

03

It connects fragmented work.

Iriz creates continuity across browser research, documents, meetings, applications, purchases and everyday decisions.

Asset guidance

Keep it recognizably Iriz.

  • Use the supplied icon without cropping, rotation, recoloring or added effects.
  • Leave clear space around the icon and keep it legible at small sizes.
  • Describe the product as local-first, not fully offline: enabled AI features send relevant requests directly to OpenAI.
  • Do not claim App Store, Setapp or direct-download availability until the relevant release is live.
