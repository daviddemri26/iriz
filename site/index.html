Local first, clearly explained

Your Mac filters first. OpenAI interprets only when needed.

iriz does not stream your entire day. It detects useful signals locally, makes selected requests directly to OpenAI with your API key, and keeps the useful results encrypted on your Mac.

01

Detect selectively

Frame comparison, OCR, silence detection, privacy exclusions, and local relevance filters remove routine activity before advanced interpretation.

02

Interpret only what matters

Relevant text, a key image, an audio segment, or bounded answer evidence may go directly from your Mac to OpenAI.

03

Remember, answer, act

Useful events return to encrypted local memory, Ask iriz answers with sources, and meaningful loose ends become Actions.

Does iriz run a local generative model? No. Apple frameworks handle preprocessing and local search; OpenAI models handle advanced language, image, and speech understanding.

Local first, by design

Your memory should belong to you.

iriz is built around explicit boundaries, local encryption, and deliberate controls. There is no iriz cloud account, ad network, or developer-operated analytics pipeline in the current app.

  • ✓Encrypted on your Mac. Memory and search are protected with AES-GCM; encryption keys live in macOS Keychain.
  • ✓Raw evidence expires. Screenshots and audio are encrypted locally and automatically removed after 24 hours.
  • ✓You choose the boundaries. Pause instantly, manage permissions, and exclude apps, domains, or window titles.
  • ✓Sensitive inputs stay out. iriz never captures keystrokes, clipboard contents, or camera video.
  • ✓AI requests stay selective. Your OpenAI key remains in Keychain, requests go directly from your Mac, and Responses API analysis uses store: false.

Under the hood

Native, selective, and technically honest.

iriz combines on-device filtering with targeted OpenAI calls. There is no browser wrapper, no iriz account, and no developer-operated server between your Mac and OpenAI.

01

Native observationScreenCaptureKit observes selected screen changes. Microphone and meeting audio remain separate, optional channels.

02

On-device filteringApple Vision OCR, local frame comparison, exclusions, and voice-activity detection remove repetition and silence before any AI request.

03

Task-aware interpretationRoutine classification, speech processing, and intensive reasoning use distinct visual activity levels and task-specific model routing.

04

Encrypted local memoryStructured memory and raw media use AES-GCM encryption. API and encryption keys stay in macOS Keychain.

05

Local-first recallSQLite FTS5 and Apple NLEmbedding rank local results first. Only a bounded set of relevant events is used to compose an answer.

WHEN NOTHING CHANGESZero AI calls

An unchanged screen and silence do not trigger OpenAI.

WHEN AI IS NEEDEDDirect, limited requests

Selected OCR, transcript, URL context, or a key image goes from your Mac to OpenAI with store: false.

WHEN CONTEXT IS PRIVATENo sensitive source detail

The indicator can show Private without exposing the app, domain, or secure field that caused it.

WHAT NEVER GETS CAPTUREDNo keys, clipboard, or camera

iriz does not record keystrokes, clipboard contents, or camera video.
