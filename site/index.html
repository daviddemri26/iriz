How it works

A memory layer for the work between the apps.

Notes only help when you remember to take them. Iriz notices the context around your real work, filters out the ordinary and preserves the moments you will want later.

01

Observe meaningful change

Iriz uses native macOS permissions to notice meaningful screen changes and, when enabled, spoken moments and meetings.

02

Keep the signal

Local filtering suppresses repetition. AI interpretation turns relevant evidence into clean events instead of a noisy activity log.

03

Recall and act

Search the Journal, ask a question with citations, or move commitments through Follow Up until the loose end is closed.

Local first, by design

Your memory should belong to you.

Iriz is built around clear boundaries, local encryption and deliberate controls. There is no Iriz cloud account, ad network or analytics pipeline in the current app.

  • ✓Encrypted on your Mac. The journal and search index are protected with AES-GCM; encryption keys live in macOS Keychain.
  • ✓Raw evidence expires. Screenshots and audio are encrypted locally and automatically removed after 24 hours.
  • ✓You choose the boundaries. Pause instantly, manage permissions and exclude apps, domains or window titles.
  • ✓Sensitive inputs stay out. Iriz never captures keystrokes, clipboard contents or camera video.

Under the hood

Native, focused and technically honest.

Iriz uses modern macOS frameworks and keeps its architecture compact. There is no browser wrapper and no required third-party runtime.

01

SwiftUI + AppKitA native macOS interface and lightweight floating capsule.

02

ScreenCaptureKit + VisionEfficient capture, local frame comparison and on-device OCR.

03

SQLite FTS5 + AES-GCMFast local search inside an encrypted journal store.

04

Direct OpenAI APIYour API key stays in Keychain; relevant requests go from your Mac to OpenAI.
